Fairtaler Translate - in your browser
=====================================

Fairtaler runs completely on your own computer. Nothing needs to be
installed and no website is involved: a tiny built-in server shows the app
in your normal browser at http://localhost:47831/ (only reachable from this
computer).

Windows
  Double-click "Start Fairtaler.cmd".
  Close the black window to stop Fairtaler.
  (If Windows warns about an unknown app, choose "More info" > "Run anyway".)

Linux / macOS
  Open a terminal in this folder and run:   sh start-fairtaler.sh
  Needs Python 3 (already installed on most systems).
  Press Ctrl+C to stop Fairtaler.

Your history, saved translations and settings are kept by your browser,
on this computer only.

Offline translation (Mozilla), the Japanese helpers and the voices work
without internet. The online translation sources (Google, DuckDuckGo...)
of course need internet, and only when you choose them.
